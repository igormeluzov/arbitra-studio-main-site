Privacy Policy

Updated October 30, 2026

Website functionality

This is an informational website for Arbitra Studio. The current implementation has no contact forms, analytics scripts, advertising pixels or affiliate tracking links. The website’s own script only controls the navigation menu and funnel diagnostic tabs. It does not set cookies or store information in your browser.

Hosting and external resources

Your browser sends technical requests to the hosting provider to load and secure this website. Those requests may include your IP address, browser information and request time.

The site loads the Tailwind CSS browser library from jsDelivr and JetBrains Mono from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to those providers when requesting these resources, which can disclose technical information such as your IP address and browser details. Their own privacy practices apply. This website does not add analytics or advertising tracking through those resources.

Email correspondence

Email links open your email application. When you choose to send a message, Arbitra Studio receives your email address and the information you include. We use correspondence to respond to enquiries and discuss potential partnerships. Please do not send sensitive personal information.

Contact

For questions about this website or your correspondence, contact info@arbitra.com.
